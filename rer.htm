📖 Reading Section: Driving Lessons & Lost Keys

Mark is learning to drive. He started two weeks ago, and he takes lessons every Tuesday and Thursday. His teacher, Anna, says he is learning quickly, but Mark still feels nervous.

Mark has a problem. He often loses his keys. Last week he lost them again. He has lost his keys three times this month! Anna laughs and says, "Maybe you should keep your keys in one place."

This morning, Mark is looking for his keys again. He has been looking for twenty minutes. Finally, he finds them in his jacket. Now he can go to his driving lesson. He hopes today will be a good day.
