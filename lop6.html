Life on a Research Vessel

Marine Research Routines

Workers on a marine research vessel usually work six weeks at sea and six weeks on land. Everyone follows a strict schedule.

Sarah is on the Science Crew. She usually analyzes water samples in the lab. But today, she is collecting data on the deck because the weather is good.

Mark is the Chief Engineer. He maintains the ship's engines and reports to the Captain.

Elena is the Head Chef. She supervises the kitchen staff and prepares meals for the whole crew. The crew eats dinner at 6 PM every day.
