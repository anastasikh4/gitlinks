Carlos the Guitarist

Read the text below carefully. Then answer the comprehension questions.

Present

Hi! I'm Carlos Ramírez, and I'm a young guitarist on the streets of Madrid! I come from Mexico, but now I live in Spain because I am studying flamenco music at the Royal Conservatory. I share a small flat with two other musicians near the city centre, and I play my guitar in Plaza Mayor almost every evening. I don't have a regular job, so I earn a little money from the tourists who stop to listen. I miss my family in Mexico very much, but I have many good friends here. The conservatory has students from more than twenty countries.

Past

I was born in Guadalajara – a big, colourful city in Mexico. I moved to Madrid three years ago when I was nineteen. I wanted to leave my small town, but I didn't want to leave my parents. Luckily, I won a music scholarship, so I couldn't say "no" to that! In my first year, I felt very homesick, but soon I got used to Spanish life.

Future

Next summer, I am going to play in a real flamenco band, not just on the streets. I am going to tour around Spain with three other musicians, and after that I am going to return to Mexico for a big family concert. My dream is to become a professional flamenco guitarist one day.
