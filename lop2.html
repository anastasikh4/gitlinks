Exercise 1: Complete the dialogue

Leo: Hi, Mia. Can you help me with my History project? You are better (good) at this than me. I can't understand this text. It's more difficult (difficult) than the other chapters. It's longer (long) than the other chapters too. It has fifty pages! It's the most boring (boring) subject in this school!

Mia: Don't worry. Let's see. That's funny...

Leo: What?

Mia: Well, I don't know these words! You are looking at page 10. Our project is on page 40.

Leo: Good! I'm not the worst (bad) student in our History class!

Choose the correct answer based on the dialogue:

1. Who does Leo think is better at History?
2. How does Leo describe the chapter?
3. Why is Leo happy at the end?

Harry
1. young → Harry is younger than George.

George
5. funny → George is funnier than Harry.
6. stylish → His clothes are more stylish than his brother's.
7. organised → He's also more organised and he's a better dancer than Harry.
