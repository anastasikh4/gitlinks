📖 Saturday Morning at Home

It is Saturday. I clear the table after breakfast. My sister Emma makes the beds and I iron my T-shirts. My father waters the plants and my mother takes out the rubbish. After lunch, we vacuum the floor in the living room.

Right now, my sister is texting her friend. She loves this film. My grandpa often plays tennis at the weekend. What are you doing at the moment? I hate homework, but I usually have a snack first. I love my family.

📘 Grammar · Present Simple vs Continuous

Present Simple = facts, habits, routines, feelings. I hate homework. · She loves this film. · He often plays tennis.
Present Continuous = actions happening now, at the moment, right now. He is texting right now. · What are you doing at the moment?
⚠️ State verbs (love, hate, like, want, know) are never continuous! ✔ I hate homework.   ✘ I am hating homework.
