🧹 Chores & Time Quest

📋 Emma's Blog: Which chores do you like? Which do you hate?


I don't like washing the dishes or cleaning the kitchen at 7 pm after dinner. It's boring! I hate cooking dinner because I always burn something!

I like sweeping the floor and mopping the tiles. It makes the house look clean.

I love dusting the furniture and folding the laundry on Saturdays.

I really like watering the plants on Sunday mornings.

I don't mind tidying my room and organizing my bookshelf at the weekend.

But my favourite chore is baking cookies on my birthday — they smell delicious!


— Emma 🌻

⏰ Prepositions of Time:
📌 at → specific times: at 7 pm, at lunchtime, at the weekend
📌 on → days and dates: on Saturday, on my birthday, on Sunday mornings
