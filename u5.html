Present Simple vs Present Continuous

☕ A DAY IN THE LIFE OF A FLIGHT ATTENDANT I work as a flight attendant for a big international airline. That means I travel all over the world, but I don't work in an office. Most people think my job is easy, but I do a lot of training every year.

6:00 a.m.
I usually get up very early. Today, I am getting up at 4:00 a.m. because we are flying to Tokyo.

10:30 a.m.
We are serving meals and drinks right now. A passenger is asking me for water. Another one is watching a film.

2:00 p.m.
Most passengers are sleeping now. I am checking the cabin quietly. My colleague is talking to the pilot.

7:00 p.m.
We usually arrive in Tokyo in the evening. This week we are staying in a new hotel. I love my job!
Present Simple = routine Present Continuous = now / temporary
