Vocabulary & Everyday Conversational English
Text
Welcome to a new town! It is a beautiful neighborhood. You can visit many places to buy food, medicine, or enjoy entertainment. If you need some money, you can look around and ask a person, "Is there a bank near here?"If you like to read books, you can also ask, "How far is the library?" This helps you to find a way. Walking in a new town is fun!
1. Where do you go to buy fresh bread, cakes, and pastries?

3. Where can you find a large collection of books to read or borrow?
4. A place with a pool where you go to exercise or practice your strokes is a...
5. Where do you go to catch a flight and travel to another country?
6. "Excuse me, is there a bank near here?"
7. "Excuse me, what's the time, please?"
