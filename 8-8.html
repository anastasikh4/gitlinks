📝 Present Simple vs Present Continuous

📖 Read the dialogue

Emma: Hi, Harry. What are you doing?

Harry: I am setting the table for dinner. Why are you asking?

Emma: I need some help with tidying my room. I don't like doing it on my own. Please!

Harry: I've got my own chores, thanks!

Emma: What about George? Is he doing any work now? Can he help me?

Harry: George hates chores too! He doesn't like tidying or vacuuming or ...

Emma: OK. Never mind.
