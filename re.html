🧠 Present Simple vs Present Continuous

📖 READ THE DIALOGUE

Read the chat between two friends. Look at how they talk about routines and things happening right now!
Amy: Hey Ben! When are the kids coming back from their summer holiday?
Ben: They arrive soon! School starts on the 15th, so they are coming back a week earlier.
Amy: Oh, I see. What about Sue? Is she coming too?
Ben: No, Sue is looking for a new flat at the moment, so she is very busy this week!

EXERCISE 1 Gap-Fill

Choose the correct words to complete the sentences.
1A: When _______ back from their holiday? (the kids / come)
1B: School _______ on the 15th, so a week earlier. (start)
2A: Sue _______ for a new flat at the moment. (look)
2B: She _______ her homework every evening. (do)
3A: Listen! The birds _______ outside the window. (sing)
3B: My brother _______ coffee every morning. (drink)

EXERCISE 2 True / False

Are these statements about the dialogue true or false?
1. Amy asks when the kids are coming back from their holiday.
2. School starts on the 14th.
3. The kids are coming back a week earlier because school starts soon.
4. Sue is looking for a new job at the moment.
5. Sue is very busy this week.
6. Ben says Sue is coming back with the kids.

EXERCISE 3 Multiple Choice

Choose the correct answer about the dialogue.
1. Where are the kids coming back from?
2. When does school start?
3. What is Sue doing at the moment?
4. Why are the kids coming back a week earlier?
5. Why is Sue very busy this week?
