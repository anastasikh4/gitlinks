🗣️ Breaking the Ice · A1+

📖 Read the text

Do you feel shy when you meet new people? Don't worry! Everyone feels this way sometimes.


Here is some advice for you:


• Smile. A smile shows you are friendly.

• Find hobbies. Interesting people have interesting things to talk about.

• Be positive. Always look on the bright side. Happy people make others feel good. Everyone can benefit from positive energy.

• Listen. Ask questions and show interest in others.

• Try. Don't run away from new people. Stay and you will get used to it.


Remember, the other person might be shy too. Break the ice and start a conversation!
