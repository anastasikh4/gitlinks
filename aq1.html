Which of these words are positive? (excited, good, interested)

Match 1–6 to the correct prepositions:

1. I'm bad skiing. I often fall.

2. I'm excited my new invention. It's fantastic!

3. I'm worried Pi. I can't find him anywhere.

4. I'm good inventing things!

5. I'm scared spiders.

6. I'm interested science.

Correct!
